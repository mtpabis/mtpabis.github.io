Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-21

"rclone personal" is an OAuth client used only by its owner, for their own Google account, through the open-source rclone tool running on their own computers.

  • Google Drive data is read and written only by the owner's local rclone installation, to list, compare and copy their own files.
  • No data is collected, stored, or shared with any third party, and there is no server-side component.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only in the owner's local rclone configuration file.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
